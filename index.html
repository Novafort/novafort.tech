Product 01 · Treasury

Treasury OS

Built forBanks, microfinance institutions and financial institutions with an active treasury desk.

Treasury OS brings deal requests, authorizations, limits and payments into one controlled workspace. Instead of tracking deals across email threads and spreadsheets, your team captures each deal once, generates the deal records and documents automatically, checks it against limits, routes it for authorization and automates the payment, with a complete record at every step.

Deal captureRecord deals in a single register with consistent fields and statuses.
Smart deal generationGenerate deal records and documents automatically from incoming requests, ready for review.
AuthorizationsRoute each deal to the right authorizer with a clear decision history.
Limit controlCheck every deal against your limits before it moves forward.
Payment automationAutomate payment processing for approved deals while keeping approvals in place.
Financial risk Coming soonFinancial risk management is on the roadmap and will be added to the platform.

Product 02 · Factoring

Factoring ERP

Built forFactoring companies that want origination, servicing and collections in one system.

Factoring ERP runs the full factoring lifecycle: onboarding clients, underwriting, servicing invoices, funding, collecting from debtors and reporting on the portfolio. Every step lives in one system of record, so your team stops reconciling spreadsheets.

Origination & underwritingStructured intake and review of clients, debtors and invoices.
Invoice servicingTrack each invoice, its status and its documents from purchase to settlement.
FundingPrepare and control advances against approved invoices.
Collections & reportingFollow debtor payments and see portfolio performance at a glance.

Product 03 · Collections

AI Receivables Manager

Built forFinance and collections teams that manage many overdue invoices and customer conversations.

AI Receivables Manager helps collectors decide who to contact first and what to say. It keeps customer context in one place, records promises and exceptions, and prepares follow-ups with AI. Your team reviews and approves each message before it goes out.

Collections prioritizationA ranked list of accounts to work, based on what matters to your business.
Customer contextHistory, notes and open items for each customer, all in one view.
Promises & exceptionsTrack payment promises, disputes and special cases so nothing is lost.
AI-assisted follow-upDrafted reminders and replies that a person approves before sending.

AI B2B Sales

Built forB2B companies and sales teams that want more qualified conversations with less manual research.

AI B2B Sales finds and researches prospects, ranks them against your criteria and prepares outreach for your team. It runs on our own database of 1 million US businesses. Nothing is sent without approval, so you get the speed of automation with a person responsible for every message.

How we work

Automation with control built in.

We start with the operational process, not the technology. AI is introduced where it improves speed or judgment, while approvals and accountability remain explicit where they matter.

STEP 1

Understand the workflow

Map the process, handoffs, decisions, data and operational pain points.

STEP 2

Structure the operating model

Define roles, controls, exceptions and the system of record before automating.

STEP 3

Add automation and AI

Use software, integrations and agents where they create measurable leverage.

STEP 4

Keep humans in material decisions

Approval, risk and exception paths remain visible and auditable.
